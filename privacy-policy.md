# Privacy Policy

**ReelSense**
Last updated: June 2026

## 1. Who we are
ReelSense ("we", "our", "us") is a fishing intelligence mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the ReelSense app.

## 2. Information we collect

**Account information**
When you create an account, we collect your email address and a display name you choose.

**Location data**
We collect your approximate GPS location when you use the app to find nearby fishing spots and show accurate weather conditions. Location is only accessed when the app is open and in use. We do not track your location in the background.

**Catch logs**
When you log a catch, we collect the species, length, lure used, whether you released the fish, and optional notes and photos. This data is private to your account.

**Photos**
If you attach a photo to a catch log, the photo is stored securely in our cloud storage. Photos are private to your account.

**Usage data**
We collect basic information about how you use the app (which features you use, error logs) to improve the product. This data does not identify you personally.

## 3. How we use your information

We use your information to:
- Provide the fishing intelligence features (conditions, recommendations, spot discovery)
- Show you local fishing conditions based on your location
- Store and display your personal catch history
- Improve the accuracy of our fishing recommendations over time
- Send password reset emails when requested

We do not sell your personal information to any third party.

## 4. Third-party services & attributions

- **Weather data** provided by [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) (open-meteo.com). We send your approximate location coordinates to retrieve weather conditions. No personal identifiers are sent. Used under Open-Meteo's free tier terms which require this attribution.
- **Map and location data** © [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL). We use OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service for geocoding (converting coordinates to place names). No personal identifiers are sent.

## 5. Data retention

Your account data is retained as long as your account is active. If you delete your account through the app, all your personal data (catches, photos, profile) is permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days.

## 6. Your rights

You have the right to:
- **Access** your data — your catch history is visible in the app at any time
- **Delete** your account and all associated data — use the "Delete account" option in the You tab settings
- **Export** your catch data — use the "Export catch data (CSV)" option in the You tab settings
- **Correct** your information — update your profile in the app at any time

## 7. Children's privacy

ReelSense is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

## 8. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), row-level security on our database so users can only access their own data, and secure authentication via Supabase Auth.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
